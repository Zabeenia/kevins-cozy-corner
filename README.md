# Kevin's Cozy Corner – An Enderman Plushie

*Meet Kevin the Endermate, your tiny, grass-block-loving buddy!*

Always holding his beloved grass block, Kevin is ready to bring a little Enderman charm to your Minecraft world. Place him anywhere and give your new little friend a cozy home! 💜

💜 Why Kevin?

Why Kevin and not just another Enderman?

Well, Kevin's story began as a funny little inside joke that somehow turned into a beloved character of his own.

Unlike most Endermen, Kevin isn't particularly interested in teleporting around or causing trouble. He prefers staying home, enjoying his cozy little corner and keeping an eye on things while you're away.

He's quite happy being home alone, actually. Someone has to look after the house, after all!

And don't worry — he's got his beloved grass block to keep him company. 🌱

## ✨ Features

- **Adorable Enderman Plushie** – A tiny decorative companion with purple eyes and his beloved grass block.
- **Place Anywhere** – Give Kevin a cozy spot in your home, on a shelf or wherever he feels welcome.
- **8-Way Rotation** – Kevin automatically faces you when placed and can be rotated in 45° steps by right-clicking with an empty hand.
- **Survival Friendly** – Craft your own Kevin using simple materials.
- **Vanilla-Inspired Pixel Art** – A small, lightweight decorative addition to your Minecraft world.

## 🧵 Crafting Recipe

Arrange the following materials in a crafting table:

| Empty | Purple Dye | Empty |
|---|---|---|
| Black Wool | Gray Wool | Black Wool |
| Empty | Gray Wool | Empty |

**Ingredients:**
- 2× Black Wool
- 2× Gray Wool
- 1× Purple Dye

**Result:** 1× Kevin the Endermate
